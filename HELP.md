# GYM Workout Tracker — Help

**Created by:** its_vinitgujjar

## Getting Started

1. Open GYM Workout Tracker.
2. Complete the initial setup.
3. Open **Plan** and configure your weekly routine.
4. Open today's workout.
5. Tap **Start Workout**.
6. Enter weight, reps and RIR.
7. Use the rest timer when needed.
8. Tap **End Workout** and save.

## Cloud Sync

For cross-device synchronization, sign in with Google and use the same Google account on your phone and laptop.

## If Data Does Not Appear

- Check that both devices use the same Google account.
- Refresh the page.
- Check cloud-sync status.
- Do not clear browser/site data until synchronization has been checked.

## Workout History

Use **History** to review saved workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weight and workout details.

## Progress

Use **Progress** to review performance trends and personal records.

## Export

Use CSV export to keep a copy of your workout history.

## Troubleshooting

### Google Sign-In does not work
Check your internet connection and verify that you are using the intended Google account.

### Website looks outdated after an update
On Windows use `Ctrl + Shift + R`. On mobile, reopen the site or clear the site's cached version if necessary.

### Data is missing on another device
Verify that both devices use the same Google account before clearing any local browser data.
